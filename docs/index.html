SemEval-2027 · Task 1

Reasoning-Oriented RetrievalAcross time and conversation

Most retrieval is judged on topical relevance. RETECO asks a harder question: can a system find the evidence that matters because of when it applies, and what the conversation has already established?

Two tracks

Retrieval that has to reason.

Track 01 · 13 domains
Sub-tracks 1a, 1b

Temporal Grounded Retrieval

Evidence has to be topically relevant and temporally aligned with the query — for whole questions, and for the decomposed steps a system must work through.

Track 02 · 11 domains
Sub-tracks 2a, 2b, 2c

Conversational Retrieval

Relevance depends on what the dialogue already established. Retrieve for the current turn, generate a grounded answer, or do both end to end.
