The task

Retrieval that understands context.

RETECO moves beyond topical similarity by testing when evidence is valid, how information changes, and what a conversation has already established.

Full task definitions →
Track 01 · TEMPO

Temporal grounded retrieval

Retrieve evidence aligned with the query’s required time periods and expose the intermediate retrieval plan.

1a Retrieval1b Step-wise13 domains
Track 02 · RECOR

Conversational reasoning retrieval

Resolve prior turns, ellipsis, and implicit links to retrieve supporting evidence and generate grounded responses.

2a Retrieval2b Gold RAG2c Full RAG11 domains
