The challenge

Two contexts. One retrieval frontier.

Topical similarity is not enough. RETECO tests the context that makes evidence relevant: when it was true, how it changed, and what the conversation already established.

Track 01 · TEMPO

Temporal grounded retrieval

Retrieve passages that are both topically relevant and aligned with the query’s required time periods—then expose the intermediate retrieval plan.

Evaluation at a glance

One official retrieval metric. Deeper diagnostics.

Both tracks use nDCG@10 for clear retrieval ranking. Temporal completeness, turn depth, domain variation, and generation quality reveal why systems succeed or fail.
