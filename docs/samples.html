Track 01 sample

TEMPO temporal retrieval

The curated subset contains five queries from Bitcoin, Economics, Genealogy, History, and History of Science and Mathematics, plus all 26 distinct gold passages they reference.

Temporal queryid · 122291_22

Why was there a jump in the number of transactions per block—from roughly 2,000 to 3,000—in April–May 2023?

The query requires evidence explaining a time-localized change rather than documents that merely mention Bitcoin transactions or the year 2023.

Important scope

A format demonstration—not a new split.

  • Every record is copied from an official public release; no labels, negatives, questions, or answers were synthesized for this package.
  • The selection is intentionally diverse and inspectable, not statistically representative or domain-balanced.
  • The included documents are only the positive evidence subset. Baselines must run against the corresponding full corpus.
  • This package is not the hidden SemEval-2027 test set.
Do not benchmark on the sample corpus aloneBecause the package contains only referenced positive evidence, retrieval scores computed over it would be invalid and artificially easy.
