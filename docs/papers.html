SemEval-2027

RETECO task proposal

Accepted SemEval-2027 shared task

RETECO: A SemEval-2027 Shared Task on Reasoning-Oriented Retrieval

Abdelrahman Abdallah¹, Mohammed Ali¹, Muhammad Abdul-Mageed², Kevin Duh³, and Adam Jatowt¹

¹ University of Innsbruck · ² University of British Columbia · ³ Johns Hopkins University

The proposal defines two complementary tracks—temporally grounded retrieval and reasoning-intensive conversational retrieval—with five sub-tracks, held-out evaluation, operational baselines, and a unified retrieval-ranking protocol.

Track 01 foundation

TEMPO

arXiv:2601.09523 · 2026

TEMPO: A Realistic Multi-Domain Benchmark for Temporal Reasoning-Intensive Retrieval

Abdelrahman Abdallah, Mohammed Ali, Muhammad Abdul-Mageed, and Adam Jatowt

TEMPO introduces 1,730 complex temporal queries over 1,654,055 documents in 13 domains, 3,976 decomposed retrieval steps, and temporal completeness metrics.

Track 02 foundation

RECOR

Findings of ACL 2026 · pp. 2688–2723

RECOR: Reasoning-focused Multi-turn Conversational Retrieval Benchmark

Mohammed Ali, Abdelrahman Abdallah, Amit Agarwal, Hitesh Laxmichand Patel, and Adam Jatowt

RECOR contains 707 conversations and 2,971 turns across 11 domains, combining discourse dependencies with reasoning-dependent passage relevance.
