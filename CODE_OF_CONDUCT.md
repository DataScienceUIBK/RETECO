# Community Code of Conduct

RETECO is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and scientifically constructive
community. Participants, organizers, and contributors are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- welcome participants regardless of background, identity, institution, or
  level of experience;
- critique systems and ideas rather than people;
- protect private task data and participant information;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, and personal attacks;
- report conflicts of interest and suspected benchmark integrity issues.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the lead organizer at
`abdelrahman.abdallah@uibk.ac.at`. Organizers may remove content, restrict
community access, or refer serious incidents to SemEval leadership and the
relevant institution.
